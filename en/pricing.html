How much does a corporate film, commercial or drone shoot cost in Gaziantep?

At AirviewTR, corporate and factory films start at 29,900 TL, commercials at 19,900 TL, monthly social media video packages at 14,900 TL and drone filming at 6,900 TL. Prices include VAT, are current as of October 2026 and valid until 31 March 2027. Every service has three packages; the middle one is the most popular scope. A Standard factory film costs 54,900 TL and includes a full shoot day, one executive interview, a 2-3 minute main film, a 60-second cut and three vertical cut-downs.

Travel within Gaziantep province is free; a daily travel fee of 1,900 TL applies for Kahramanmaraş and Kilis, 3,900 TL for Şanlıurfa, Adıyaman and Hatay, and 5,900 TL for Adana and Mersin. Licensed music, colour grading and unlimited digital usage rights are included in every package. The final price is confirmed in a written, itemised quote after a short discovery call; nothing outside the quote is added later.

How much is a corporate film in Gaziantep?

At AirviewTR corporate and factory films start at 29,900 TL (VAT included, October 2026). Compact 29,900 TL, Standard 54,900 TL with a full shoot day and executive interview, Export 94,900 TL with two shoot days and an English version.

How much is drone filming? Is there an hourly rate?

Drone filming starts at 6,900 TL. Half day 14,900 TL, full day with ground camera 24,900 TL. We charge per package because editing, colour and music are included; each extra hour is 2,500 TL.

Do prices include VAT?

Yes. All prices on the site include VAT. Divide by 1.2 for the pre-VAT amount; for example, the 54,900 TL package is 45,750 TL before VAT.

Do you film outside Gaziantep?

Yes. Travel within Gaziantep province is free. A daily travel fee of 1,900 TL applies up to 100 km, 3,900 TL for 100-200 km and 5,900 TL for 200-300 km; further provinces are quoted.

How is payment made?

50% upfront to confirm the date and 50% on delivery. Monthly packages are paid at the start of each month. A corporate invoice is issued.

How long are these prices valid?

Prices were updated on 13 October 2026 and are valid until 31 March 2027. Signed quotes don't change within their validity period.
