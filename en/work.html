What is on this page?

These are films AirviewTR filmed and edited from Gaziantep: a short and an 8-minute corporate film for Teknoçim Makine; brand, launch and social films for Beyaz Group İnşaat, KNZ İnşaat, Yaşar Group, Erkan Boz İnşaat, Ege Kalıp Sistemleri, abl yapı and MAK Life İnşaat; AI-assisted product films in three languages for Yılmazkar Group's Shelly Clean brand; and an aerial city film of Adana. Each film has its own page describing the brief, how it was made and what was delivered.

Factory and corporate films

Teknoçim Makine's factory film and its full 8-minute corporate version are real filming end to end: the viewer of a factory film wants to see the real line.

Construction and real estate films

Films for Beyaz Group İnşaat, KNZ İnşaat, Yaşar Group, Erkan Boz İnşaat, Ege Kalıp Sistemleri, abl yapı and MAK Life İnşaat. Launch films start from drone footage we shot on site; when the building isn't finished yet, renders and AI-assisted visualisation show it complete.

Product films and commercials

New product and scent films every month for Yılmazkar Group's Shelly Clean brand. Packaging comes from real product photos, scenes are AI-assisted, and editing, colour and sound are done by our team.
