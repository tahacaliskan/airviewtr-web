Commercial or corporate film?

A commercial sells a product, a campaign or one message; it is short and runs with a media budget. A corporate film introduces the company itself, is longer and is used for years. One drives a quick decision, the other builds trust. Where the film will run decides which one you need.

How is a commercial planned?

  1. One-sentence idea: what should stay with the viewer?
  2. Script: hook, development and close; the first two seconds written separately.
  3. Storyboard: framing, duration and text of every scene.
  4. Shoot: product, location or talent, with vertical and horizontal framing in mind.
  5. Edit and sound: rhythm, music, sound design and on-screen text.
  6. Versions: 30, 15 and 6 second cuts, vertical and horizontal.

Are talent, location and media budget included?

No. Talent, models, studio and location rental and the media budget are not included; they are quoted at cost as separate lines. We coordinate casting, schedule and permits.
