What is AirviewTR?

What is AirviewTR and what does it do?

AirviewTR is a video production and advertising agency based in Gaziantep, Türkiye. It produces commercials, corporate and factory films, product films, drone footage and social media videos for manufacturers, construction and real estate developers, hotels, restaurants and brands. The same team writes the script, films on the ground and from the air, edits and grades the film, so the shoot day matches what was agreed at the planning table. Where it fits, AirviewTR also produces AI-assisted commercials built on real product photos and drone footage; the Shelly Clean product films and KNZ İnşaat's render-to-reality Reels were made this way.

Founder Taha Çalışkan is an IHA-1 certified commercial drone pilot and every flight is covered by third-party liability insurance. Corporate films start at 29,900 TL, commercials at 19,900 TL and drone filming at 6,900 TL (VAT included); every package is listed on the pricing page. Small jobs are delivered in 3-5 working days, corporate films in 7-15 depending on the package. English, Arabic and Azerbaijani versions are available, and quote requests are answered the same day.

Process

A shoot day with no surprises.

01 · Discovery

Discovery call

Audience, where the film will run and the date are agreed. If needed, we visit the location.

02 · Plan

Script and shot plan

Story, scene list, ground and aerial balance, an hour-by-hour schedule and permits for your approval.

03 · REC

Shoot

A team of 2-4 films the ground and aerial plan on the same day without stopping your work.

04 · Delivery

Edit and delivery

First cut, revision rounds by package, colour and sound. The main film and vertical cut-downs are delivered together.

Frequently asked.

All questions
How much is a corporate film in Gaziantep?

Corporate and factory films start at 29,900 TL (VAT included, October 2026). Standard with a full shoot day, executive interview and 2-3 minute film is 54,900 TL; Export with two days and an English version is 94,900 TL. All packages are on the pricing page.

How much is drone filming?

From 6,900 TL: one location, up to 1.5 hours on site, 15 edited photos and a 30-second edited clip. Half day 14,900 TL, full day with ground camera 24,900 TL, VAT included.

Do prices include VAT?

Yes. All prices on the site include VAT. Divide by 1.2 for the pre-VAT amount. A corporate invoice is issued.

How long does delivery take?

By package: drone and small jobs 3-5 working days, commercials and product films 5-10, corporate and factory films 7-15. Express delivery halves the time for an extra fee.

How many revisions do I get?

1 to 3 revision rounds depending on the package; the number is written on the package card. Notes are sent together in one round. Each extra round is 1,900 TL.

Is your drone pilot licensed and are flights insured?

Yes. Flights are flown by IHA-1 certified commercial drone pilot Taha Çalışkan, and every flight is covered by third-party liability insurance.

Do you film outside Gaziantep?

Yes. Travel within Gaziantep province is free. Daily travel fees are 1,900 TL for Kahramanmaraş and Kilis, 3,900 TL for Şanlıurfa, Adıyaman and Hatay, and 5,900 TL for Adana and Mersin. Further provinces are quoted.
