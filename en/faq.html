Prices and payment

How much is a corporate film in Gaziantep?

Corporate and factory films start at 29,900 TL (VAT included, October 2026). Standard with a full shoot day, executive interview and 2-3 minute film is 54,900 TL; Export with two days and an English version is 94,900 TL. All packages are on the pricing page.

How much is drone filming?

From 6,900 TL: one location, up to 1.5 hours on site, 15 edited photos and a 30-second edited clip. Half day 14,900 TL, full day with ground camera 24,900 TL, VAT included.

How much is a commercial?

From 19,900 TL. The Commercial package with concept and storyboard is 44,900 TL and the two-concept Campaign 84,900 TL, VAT included. Talent, studio and media budget are separate.

Do prices include VAT?

Yes. All prices on the site include VAT. Divide by 1.2 for the pre-VAT amount. A corporate invoice is issued.

How is payment made?

50% upfront confirms the date and 50% is paid on delivery. Monthly packages are paid at the start of each month.

Process and delivery

How long does delivery take?

By package: drone and small jobs 3-5 working days, commercials and product films 5-10, corporate and factory films 7-15. Express delivery halves the time for an extra fee.

How many revisions do I get?

1 to 3 revision rounds depending on the package; the number is written on the package card. Notes are sent together in one round. Each extra round is 1,900 TL.

Who writes the script?

We write the script, shot list and interview questions; you approve them. If you already have a text or idea, we develop it.

Do you work in English?

Yes. Calls, quotes and delivery can be in English, and films can be delivered with English voice-over or subtitles.

Drone and permits

Is your drone pilot licensed and are flights insured?

Yes. Flights are flown by IHA-1 certified commercial drone pilot Taha Çalışkan, and every flight is covered by third-party liability insurance.

Do you need a permit for drone filming?

Most open areas need no extra permit. Near airports, military zones, some public areas and over crowds a permit is required and is obtained before the shoot.

Which equipment do you use?

Sony A7C II on the ground and DJI Mini 5 Pro in the air; aerial footage is recorded in 4K and 10-bit D-Log M. Interviews use lavalier microphones.

Rights, files and formats

Who owns the usage rights?

Delivered films and cut-downs can be used on all digital channels without time limit. TV and cinema broadcast require a separate licence and are quoted.

Can we get the raw footage?

Yes. All raw files can be delivered via cloud link for an extra 4,900 TL; the scope of use is written into the contract.

Do you make English or Arabic versions?

Yes. Extra subtitle languages are 2,900 TL each and extra voice-over languages 9,900 TL each. We make English, Arabic and Azerbaijani versions.

Service area

Do you film outside Gaziantep?

Yes. Travel within Gaziantep province is free. Daily travel fees are 1,900 TL for Kahramanmaraş and Kilis, 3,900 TL for Şanlıurfa, Adıyaman and Hatay, and 5,900 TL for Adana and Mersin. Further provinces are quoted.

About AirviewTR

What is AirviewTR?

AirviewTR is a video production and advertising agency in Gaziantep, Türkiye. It produces commercials, corporate and factory films, product films, social media video, construction films, event filming, drone filming and editing. Its founder is Taha Çalışkan.

Do you use AI to make videos?

Where it fits. We build AI-assisted scenes from real product photos and drone footage shot on site, and finish them with editing, colour and sound. The quote states which method is used.
