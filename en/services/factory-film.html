How is a factory film shot, how long does it take and what does it cost?

AirviewTR produces factory films for manufacturers in Gaziantep's organised industrial zones with ground filming, drone filming, an executive interview and editing in one team. A single plant is usually filmed in one full day: production line and quality control in the morning, warehouse, offices and the aerial view of the plant in the afternoon. The shoot follows the plant's health and safety rules; drone flights are flown by an IHA-1 certified pilot and insured.

Prices include VAT: Compact 29,900 TL, Standard 54,900 TL with a full shoot day, interview and a 2-3 minute film, and Export 94,900 TL with two shoot days and Turkish and English voice-over. Delivery takes 7-15 working days. The film for Teknoçim Makine presents its 3,750 m² plant and roughly 300 tonnes of monthly capacity in Turkish and English.

Why is a factory film a different job?

A production floor is loud, moving and governed by safety rules. Lines run at set times, so we build the schedule around the shift plan and film from outside the safety line without changing the operator's work. Helmets, vests and safety markings must also look right on camera.

The audience is different too: a purchasing manager, distributor or engineer abroad wants capacity, quality process and delivery reliability more than machine brands. The script follows those questions.

What should a factory film show?

  • The whole site: location in the industrial zone, roofs, loading bays and warehouse, in one aerial shot.
  • The line: flow from raw material to product, machines and close-up details.
  • Quality: measurement, testing, laboratory and certificates.
  • Capacity: floor area, headcount, monthly output; figures come from you and are never invented.
  • People and shipping: engineers, operators, an executive interview, packing and loading.

Factory films for export

For foreign buyers the key decision is language. The Export package includes Turkish and English voice-over or subtitles in two languages, a 60-second trade fair cut and a silent loop. Arabic and Azerbaijani subtitles can be added for 2,900 TL per language.

FAQ

Frequently asked.

How much does a factory film cost?

From 29,900 TL. Standard is 54,900 TL and Export 94,900 TL, VAT included. Multi-plant or multi-city jobs are quoted.

How long does filming take?

A single-plant factory is usually filmed in one full day; small plants in half a day; several plants or two interviews take two days.

Do we need to stop production?

No. We schedule around shifts and film running lines from outside the safety line.

Is travel within the industrial zone charged?

No. Travel within Gaziantep province, including all organised industrial zones, is free.
