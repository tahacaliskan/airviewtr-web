What can one shoot day produce for a restaurant?

Photos for Google Business Profilee, consistent dish images for delivery apps, weekly vertical videos for Instagram and a short atmosphere film for the website, all with the same light and plating.

Why food looks flat in photos

Usually it is the light, not the food. Soft side light brings out texture, steam and shine. In video, movement creates appetite: syrup poured on dessert, bread pulled from the oven. We plan those moments and capture them in one take.
