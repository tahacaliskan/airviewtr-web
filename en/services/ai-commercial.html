What is an AI-assisted commercial and how does AirviewTR make one?

An AI-assisted commercial is a short film in which some or all scenes are generated with AI video tools, while the product, logo and location come from a real source. AirviewTR, a video production and advertising agency in Gaziantep, starts from real product photos, drone footage shot on site and the project's architectural renders. The team writes the idea, script and storyboard; generated shots are selected one by one, and any frame that distorts the product or building is left out. Editing, colour grading, music, sound design and on-screen text are finished with conventional post-production.

The product films for Yılmazkar Group's Shelly Clean brand were made this way and delivered in Turkish, Arabic and Azerbaijani. For KNZ İnşaat and Yaşar Group projects, real drone footage, renders and AI-assisted visualisation were combined to show the finished building before construction ended.

What exactly does AI do in these films?

AI generates scenes that would be expensive or impossible to film: a product in many settings, a building rising from its plot, a mood that changes with the season. The product packaging and logo always come from a real photo and are never altered; architecture stays true to the renders.

Product films: the Shelly Clean example

Shelly needed several products, scents and languages every month. Keeping the real product image fixed and generating the scenes made a steady stream of films possible at one quality level, finished with our own edit, colour and sound.

When is AI not the right choice?

When the viewer needs to see the real thing: a factory film, an executive interview, a team at work. In those jobs we recommend real filming and say so in the quote.

FAQ

Frequently asked.

Do you make commercials with AI?

Yes, where it fits. We start from real product photos, drone footage and renders; script, selection, editing, colour and sound are done by our team.

Will my product or logo be distorted?

No. Packaging and logo come from the real product photo, and any generated frame that changes them is discarded.

How is the price set?

By scope: number of films, lengths, languages and whether real filming is added. You receive a written quote.
