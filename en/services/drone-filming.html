How much does drone filming cost in Gaziantep, and who can fly?

AirviewTR films aerial video and photos of land, buildings, factories, construction sites, hotels and events in Gaziantep. Flights are flown by Taha Çalışkan, an IHA-1 certified commercial drone pilot, and every flight is covered by third-party liability insurance. Footage is recorded in 4K and 10-bit D-Log M on a DJI Mini 5 Pro and delivered edited and colour graded. In areas that require permission, such as near airports, the flight permit is obtained in advance.

Prices include VAT: Quick, one location and up to 1.5 hours on site, 6,900 TL; Half day, up to 4 hours and two locations, 14,900 TL; Full day with a ground camera, 8 hours, 24,900 TL. Every package includes an edited clip and edited photos; each extra hour is 2,500 TL. Delivery takes 3-7 working days.

What is drone filming used for?

  • Land: boundaries, road access and surrounding development for sales listings.
  • Factories and plants: roofs, loading areas and location in the industrial zone.
  • Construction sites: monthly progress from fixed angles.
  • Hotels and real estate: pool, garden, view and location.
  • City and region films: like our aerial film of Adana.

Do you need a permit to fly a drone?

Commercial drone filming requires a certified pilot, a registered aircraft and third-party liability insurance. Near airports, military zones, some public areas and over crowds an additional permit is needed and must be obtained before the shoot.
