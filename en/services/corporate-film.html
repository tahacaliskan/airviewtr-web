What is a corporate film and who needs one?

A corporate film explains in a few minutes what a company does, how it works and why it should be chosen. A commercial sells one product or campaign; a corporate film introduces the company itself and is used for years on the website, in presentations and at trade fairs.

It is most useful before a first meeting with a new or foreign customer, at trade fairs, dealer meetings and investor presentations, for recruitment and when a website is relaunched.

Which scenes go into a corporate film?

  • Opening and location: an aerial view of the building or site, so the viewer knows where they are within five seconds.
  • The work itself: production, service or process in close-ups.
  • People: the team, managers and a short interview; the fastest way to build trust.
  • Proof: capacity, certificates, references or completed projects, shown as on-screen figures.
  • Close: logo, contact and one sentence of promise.

Where is a corporate film used?

A corporate film is a set of files, not one file: the main film on the website and YouTube, a 60-second cut for LinkedIn and email, and vertical cut-downs for Reels, TikTok and Shorts. We plan horizontal and vertical framing on the same shoot day, so one budget covers every channel.

FAQ

Frequently asked.

How long should a corporate film be?

For websites and presentations 2-3 minutes works for most companies. On social media a 60-second cut performs better, and a silent 1-2 minute loop works best at trade fairs.

How long does delivery take?

Compact 7, Standard 10 and Comprehensive 15 working days after the shoot, including revision rounds.

Can you make English or Arabic versions?

Yes. The Comprehensive package includes English. Extra subtitle languages are 2,900 TL each and extra voice-over languages 9,900 TL each.

Is drone filming included?

Yes. All three corporate film packages include drone filming with an IHA-1 certified pilot and third-party liability insurance.
