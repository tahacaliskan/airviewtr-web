Taha Çalışkan

Founder of AirviewTR; director, editor and IHA-1 certified commercial drone pilot. Lives and works in Gaziantep.

Taha Çalışkan is the founder of AirviewTR, a video production agency in Gaziantep, and of the web design studio Calisqa Software. He plans films for manufacturers, construction companies, product brands and cities; films on the ground with a Sony A7C II and from the air with a DJI Mini 5 Pro as an IHA-1 certified commercial drone pilot; and edits and grades the films himself.

His work includes a Turkish and English factory film for Teknoçim Makine, drone and launch films for KNZ İnşaat and Yaşar Group projects, and AI-assisted product films in three languages for Shelly Clean.
